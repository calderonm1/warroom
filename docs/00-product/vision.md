# Product vision

## One-liner

**War Room** is a fair, modern, real-time grand strategy game played on a living 3D globe, built for the phone in your pocket and the check-ins in your day.

## Vision statement

Give strategy players the depth of Hearts of Iron and the persistent multiplayer drama of Conflict of Nations, without the two things that drive them away: pay-to-win spending and the need to be online around the clock. A War Room match unfolds over days. You command it in five-minute check-ins, and you win it through planning, diplomacy and better decisions, never a bigger wallet.

## Pillars

Every feature must strengthen at least one pillar and weaken none.

| ID | Pillar | Means | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | **Decisions over upkeep** | Every check-in has meaningful choices; standing orders and automation handle routine work | Micromanagement, or "be online at 3 a.m. or lose" |
| P2 | **Fair fight** | Skill, coordination and planning decide matches; spending buys style and convenience | Selling units, stats or resources in ranked play |
| P3 | **The world is the board** | The 3D globe is the primary interface: readable, tactile, beautiful at every zoom | A spinning globe bolted onto a flat-map game |
| P4 | **Diplomacy is a weapon** | Coalitions, treaties, intel and betrayal are as important as armies | Chat as an afterthought |
| P5 | **Respect the player's time** | Matches end decisively, inactive players are replaced by AI, and onboarding is fast | Matches that rot into empty AI maps |

## Target players

| Segment | Description | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genre veterans | Played Supremacy/CoN, HOI4, Diplomacy; frustrated by pay-to-win | Depth, fair ranked play, coalition tools |
| Busy strategists | Adults 25 to 45 who love strategy but cannot commit hours per sitting | Check-in friendly design, notifications, standing orders |
| Curious newcomers | Drawn in by the globe visuals and geopolitics theme | Fast onboarding, AI-practice matches, clear UI |

## Success measures (launch targets, to be validated in beta)

| ID | Metric | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KPI-01 | D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 40% / 18% / 8% (top 20% of 4X benchmarks) |
| KPI-02 | Match completion rate (players active at match end ÷ players at start) | ≥ 45% |
| KPI-03 | Store rating | ≥ 4.5 with "fair" as a top positive review theme |
| KPI-04 | Sessions per DAU per day | 6 to 10 |
| KPI-05 | Payer conversion | ≥ 3% with no ranked-power sales |
| KPI-06 | Crash-free sessions | ≥ 99.8% |

## Non-goals (for now)

- Real-time tactical battle control (no unit micro in battles)
- Single-player campaign beyond tutorial and AI-practice matches
- Console platforms
- User-generated maps (a possible post-launch content pack feature)
