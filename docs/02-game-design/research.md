# Research and doctrines

| ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| GDD-RES-001 | Research items unlock units, unit upgrades (levels 1 to 4), buildings, building levels and nation-wide modifiers. |
| GDD-RES-002 | **Day-gating:** each item has a minimum match day. This prevents early tech rushes and keeps the game's arc readable. |
| GDD-RES-003 | Research slots: 2 at start, 3 from Day 5 (via research). Items take game hours to days. |
| GDD-RES-004 | Costs: treasury plus electronics (and rare earths for high tiers). Tech parks speed research. |
| GDD-RES-005 | **Doctrines** (choose one at match start; changeable once after Day 10 at a cost): *Western* (quality: +air and stealth, higher costs), *Eastern* (mass: +artillery and armor, cheaper units), *Asymmetric* (+drones, EW, special forces, morale resilience). They modify stats by ±10 to 20% and unlock 1 to 2 doctrine-specific units. |
| GDD-RES-006 | Research is visible to coalition members (coordination), hidden from others unless spied on. |
| GDD-RES-007 | Purchasable items never grant or speed research in ranked (ADR-0010). |
